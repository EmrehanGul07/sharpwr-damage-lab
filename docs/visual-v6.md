# Rift Studio 6.0

The old Ezreal AA and Q shared too much of their pose and projectile silhouette. AA now uses a quick bent-elbow gold bolt. Q extends the gauntlet, winds up with cyan energy, sends a long white-core arrow, and creates a wider impact burst. W travels as a gold ring and R as a broad gold crescent. Projectile centers follow their frozen launch direction at constant height.

The circular inspection platform is replaced with an original stone lane, foliage, river, towers and contact shadows. Rift camera is the default; character, spell and tactical views remain available. High WebGL quality adds restrained bloom. A decimated, CPU-skinned Ezreal preview retains the Blender animation in browsers without WebGL.

Practice mode supports a movement joystick, tap-to-move, skill-button hold/drag aiming, Q/W/E/R keys and Space for AA. Rank-1 database cooldowns prevent repeated casting; authored cast recovery prevents overlapping actions. Nine existing movement profiles drive physical dashes, blinks, jumps and recoil. The minimap tracks hero and target. AA/Q comparison and the staged 20-second fight remain inspection tools.

This is an original visual sandbox. It does not calculate damage or simulate resources, skill resets or exact game timings. Ezreal has eight Blender-baked clips and a 17-bone skinned model. The other 22 champions retain their earlier original stylized rigs. Build Lab and the frozen Tier List retain their behavior.

Validation covers deterministic animation and flight, all 23 fight schedules, cooldown locks, CPU skinning, finite arena transforms, bounded arena mesh counts and dash completion across skipped frames. Generated GLBs, Blender sources and downloadable packages are rebuilt by the repository workflow.
